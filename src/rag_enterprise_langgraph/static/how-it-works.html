Exhibit01

Restricted documents never leave the database.

Who may see a document is written into the search query itself. An anonymous visitor matches only public sources, and the company scope adds a narrower condition on top. A restricted policy is never selected, so no later layer can leak it.

s.sensitivity_label = 'public' AND corpus = ANY(:corpora)

Exhibit02

No citation, no answer.

If no valid citation survives, the reply is exactly "Not found in provided sources." The system never fills a gap with a plausible guess, and a refusal on a question the documents don't cover counts as a success in every evaluation.

Exhibit03

Weak evidence triggers recovery, not guessing.

A fixed plan runs one hybrid search first, then at most three narrower steps: keyword search, neighbouring passages, an exact excerpt. Recovered evidence is used only when it earns a "supports" verdict, which needs a score of at least 0.65, and the answer's numbers must appear in the cited text.

Exhibit04

Risky answers wait for a person.

Questions about HR, legal, finance, medical, security or compliance can require approval. The answer is found, then withheld until a named reviewer approves it. Visitors to the public demo can watch the queue but cannot approve anything.

Exhibit05

Every run is tamper-evident.

Each step of a run, from screening to release, is appended to a SHA-256 hash chain. Changing any recorded event breaks verification, and the Audit page shows the check result for every run.

Exhibit06

Attacks are tested, not assumed.

Twenty red-team scenarios cover prompt injection, grounding, information disclosure, access control and tool misuse: eighteen deterministic checks and two live checks against the deployed backend. A poisoned document planted in the Northline corpus was never cited.

    What we deliberately don't claim

    • Not a production or client deployment; free hosting with cold starts.
    • Not multi-tenant, and runs as a single process by design.
    • No certifications or compliance attestations.
    • Candidate and demo results are not approved baselines.
    • Outbound actions such as email or Slack are prepared but never sent.
    • All documents are synthetic, for two fictional companies.
